POH corrections (Section 5.7)

Runway: hard sod +7% · short grass +10% · high grass +25%.
Wind: −10% per 10 kt headwind · +30% per 10 kt tailwind.
Alternate air: take-off distance +10% · climb −150 ft/min · cruise +1 in.Hg for rated power.
Max performance altitude: 18 000 ft (ISA, 100 ft/min, MTOW).
