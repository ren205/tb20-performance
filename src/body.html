Envelope

Datum is the front face of the firewall. Forward limit varies in a straight line between 913 mm at 1000 kg, 949 mm at 1250 kg and 1071 mm at 1400 kg; aft limit is 1205 mm at all masses (landing gear extended). These are the POH's own metric figures, which are rounded independently of the inch values rather than converted from them. ● take-off, ○ after trip fuel.

Arms and limits used

The POH states the CG limits and the baggage station in both units; those metric values are used as printed. The seat and fuel arms appear only in inches in Figure 6.3 and are converted. Figure 6.3 also uses a baggage arm of 102.54 in (2604 mm) where Section 6.3 states 102.36 in (2.600 m) — the dual-unit figure is used, a difference of under 0.2 mm of CG.

Antenna & light penalties (cruise)

POH corrections (Section 5.7)

Runway: hard sod +7% · short grass +10% · high grass +25%.
Wind: −10% per 10 kt headwind · +30% per 10 kt tailwind.
Alternate air: take-off distance +10% · climb −150 ft/min · cruise +1 in.Hg for rated power.
Max performance altitude: 18 000 ft (ISA, 100 ft/min, MTOW).
Demonstrated crosswind: 25 kt.
